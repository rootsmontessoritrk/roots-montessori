01 · Who We Are
“The child is both a hope and a promise for mankind.”
Inspired by the Montessori philosophy

A school designed around the child.

At ROOTS, we believe the early years deserve more than a busy classroom. They deserve time, beauty, order, movement, meaningful work and adults who truly observe the child.

Our environment is carefully prepared so children can choose, repeat, practise and master — building the quiet confidence that becomes a foundation for life.
